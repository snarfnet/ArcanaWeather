Arcana Weather - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-02

Data Collection

This app collects location data (GPS) solely to display weather information for your current position. Location data is sent to Open-Meteo API and is not stored on our servers.

Local Storage

Journal entries and app preferences are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data is not transmitted to any server.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data according to Google's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Open-Meteo (weather data): Terms
  • Google AdMob (advertising): Privacy Policy

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the App Store support page.
